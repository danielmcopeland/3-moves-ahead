For parents of babies 0 to 24 months

Help your baby move, play and grow, one small step at a time.

3 Moves Ahead is a nine-module program that shows you how your baby learns to move, and gives you simple, playful ways to support the next milestone during everyday moments.

Why movement matters

Your child can already do so many things.

Think about how much they have changed since the day they were born. Every roll, reach and wobble is your baby learning how their body works.

Babies grow in four connected areas: motion, cognition, communication and emotion. 3 Moves Ahead focuses on motion, because movement is how babies explore the world, and it feeds every other kind of learning.

  • Understand what your baby is working on right now
  • Offer the "just-right" challenge, not too easy and not too hard
  • Turn feeding, bath time and floor time into practice moments

How it works

Three moves, repeated with love.

Each module follows the same simple rhythm so the program fits into real family life.

1

Learn

Watch a short lesson on how children learn to move, what to look for, and why it matters for your child's development.

2

Play

Try a hands-on activity that gives your baby a just-right challenge. No special equipment, just the things you already have at home.

3

Celebrate

Notice progress, track milestones from newborn to 24 months, and celebrate the small wins that add up to big changes.

A note for families: 3 Moves Ahead is designed to support, not replace, guidance from your paediatrician or therapist. If you have concerns about your child's development, please talk to your care team.
