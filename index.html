For parents of children 0 to 36 months

Help your child move, play, and grow, one small step at a time.

3 Moves Ahead is a nine-module program that shows you how your child learns to move, and gives you simple, playful ways to support the next milestone during everyday moments.

Short lessons, real-life activities, and a milestone guide, plus a community of parents and experts.
Why movement matters

Your child can already do so many things.

Think about how much they have changed since the day they were born. Every roll, reach, and wobble is your child learning how their body works.

Children grow in four connected areas: motion, cognition, communication, and emotion. 3 Moves Ahead focuses on motion, because movement is how children explore the world, and it feeds every other kind of learning.

  • Understand what your child is working on right now
  • Offer the "just-right" challenge, not too easy and not too hard
  • Turn feeding, bath time, and floor time into practice moments

How it works

Three moves, repeated with love.

Each module follows the same simple rhythm so the program fits into real family life.

1

Learn

Watch a short lesson on how children learn to move, what to look for, and why it matters for your child's development.

2

Play

Try a hands-on activity that gives your child a "just-right" challenge. No special equipment, just the things you already have at home.

3

Celebrate

Notice progress, track milestones from 0 to 36 months, and celebrate the small wins that add up to big changes.

A note for families: 3 Moves Ahead is designed to support, not replace, guidance from your pediatrician or therapist. If you have concerns about your child's development, please talk to your care team.

The program

Nine modules that grow with you.

Start with how children learn, build up your parenting toolkit, and finish by putting it all together with music.

Each module takes just a few minutes to complete and comes with a homework activity you can start the same day.

2

3

4

Positive Parenting for Motor Learning

5

Overcoming Challenges in Motor Learning

What to do when you feel stuck or frustrated?

6

Celebrating Success

Notice and reward progress so learning stays joyful.

7

Make Learning Part of Your Daily Routine

Fold practice into meals, bath time, and play time.

8

Parenting Self-Care

9

Putting It All Together With Music

Community

Parents learn best from other parents.

The app is one half of 3 Moves Ahead. The other half is people: families working on the very same milestones, movement experts guiding the conversation, and the care team already near you.

Online discussions

Connect with parents just like you, day and night.

Peer-to-peer group sessions

Meet other families in small live groups, moderated by movement experts who keep the advice sound and make sure every parent gets a turn.

Community resources near you

Find the programs, clinics, and support in your area, and get help taking what you have learned to the people who care for your child.

Connecting you to your local healthcare system. Knowing what your child is working on is one thing; getting the right support for them is another. Our community resources point you toward the early-intervention programs, therapists, and clinics near you, and help you arrive with the questions worth asking.
